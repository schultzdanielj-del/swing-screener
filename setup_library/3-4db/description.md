# 3-4 Day Bounce (3-4DB) — Short Setup

## Summary
Stock in a downtrend bounces weakly over 3-4 days, then fails and resumes the downtrend. The bounce is a low-quality relief rally — a shorting opportunity, not a reversal.

## Key Characteristics
- Stock is in a clear downtrend (below declining 50SMA / 200SMA)
- Weak bounce lasting 3-4 days — low volume, small-bodied candles
- Bounce typically reaches or stalls near a declining short-term MA (8EMA or 21EMA)
- Failure candle confirms the bounce is over — short entry

## What Makes It Actionable vs NMS
- **Actionable:** Bounce is already stalling or showing a failure candle at resistance (MA or prior level). Entry could be next session.
- **NMS (Need More Sideways):** Bounce is only on day 1-2, or hasn't yet reached a logical resistance area.
