====================================================================================================
INTERPRETATION GUIDE
====================================================================================================

Read columns:
  per-off rand%: fraction of random bars that fit the example envelope AT that
    single offset across all 6 axes. Low = envelope is tight at that offset.
  cum AND rand%: fraction of random bars still passing after including offsets 0..k.
    Monotonically decreasing. This is the ACTUAL filter pass rate if you cut at k.
  carve Δ: how much this offset subtracted from the running pass rate (in pp).
    Big Δ = this offset contributes real incremental discrimination.

Candidate N definitions (all data-derived, no picked thresholds):
  N_saturate: cum AND has decayed under 1% AND the next five offsets add nothing
    meaningful. Picks where lengthening the window stops buying anything.
  N_pareto:   95% of total achievable carve is done. Pareto-optimal window length.
  N_max_delta: offset of the biggest single-offset carve step. (Diagnostic only,
    not a stopping point — marks the most discriminating single bar.)
  N_cum_half: first k where cum AND drops under 50%. Very coarse check.

Expected read: if lead-up shape is coherent over a real window, cum AND trajectory
  starts near 1.0, drops fast, and plateaus near 0. N_pareto identifies plateau.
  If cum AND stays high (> 30%) through k=100, the envelope is weak for that setup.

Dan picks which N definition to adopt; the report gives the data to pick from.
