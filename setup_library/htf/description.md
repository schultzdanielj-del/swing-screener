# High Tight Flag (HTF) — Long Setup

## Summary
Stock makes a powerful run-up (typically 50-100%+ in a short period), then consolidates in a tight, shallow flag near the highs. The breakout from the flag is the long entry.

## Key Characteristics
- Strong prior advance — big move in a compressed timeframe
- Tight consolidation near the highs — small range, orderly pullback (typically <20-25% from peak)
- Volume contracts during the flag / consolidation
- Price holds above rising short-term MAs (8EMA / 21EMA)
- Breakout on expanding volume

## What Makes It Actionable vs NMS
- **Actionable:** Flag is formed, consolidation is tight, and breakout is imminent or occurring. Entry could be next session.
- **NMS:** Flag is still forming — needs more time to tighten up or hasn't contracted enough yet.
