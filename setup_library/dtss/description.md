# Double Top Short Sell (DTSS) — Short Setup

## Summary
Stock rallies to a prior high (resistance) and fails to break through, forming a double top. The failed breakout is the short entry signal.

## Key Characteristics
- Clear prior high / resistance level
- Second rally into the same zone — can be slightly above or below the prior peak
- Rejection candle or reversal pattern at the double top level
- Volume often spikes on the failed attempt then dries up
- MAs may be flattening or starting to roll over

## What Makes It Actionable vs NMS
- **Actionable:** Second top is formed and showing rejection. Failure is confirmed or confirming. Short entry could be next session.
- **NMS:** Stock is still rallying toward the prior high — hasn't tested it yet.
